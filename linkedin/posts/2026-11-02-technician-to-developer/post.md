## Caption

I did my MCA while working as a hardware technician.

Work: installing operating systems, fixing laptops, setting up office networks.
Study: learning to become a developer.

It wasn't a glamorous switch. It was a long stretch of doing both.

Along the way came a full stack course in ASP.NET MVC with Angular. And in March 2023, my first developer role.

Today I build ASP.NET applications and ship AI features to production.

What I didn't expect: the technician years weren't wasted.

They taught me to stay calm when something is broken and a client is waiting. To ask "what changed?" before touching anything. To explain technical problems to people who don't care about the technical part.

Every one of those skills shows up in my code today.

If you switched careers into tech, what from your old job still helps you?
