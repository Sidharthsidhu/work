# Minnu (മിന്നു): character sheet

Reference image: minnu.png. Paste the CHARACTER block at the start of every prompt, word for word.

## CHARACTER (paste this every time)

Minnu, a tiny baby monkey with soft light-brown fur, a pale pink face, big round dark-brown eyes,
and two small pigtails on top of her head, each tied with a pink flower hair clip with a yellow
centre. She wears mustard-yellow denim overall shorts with two brown buttons and a front pocket,
and a pink-and-white bead bracelet on one wrist. Photorealistic, soft warm light.

## Fixed (never change)

- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 8-15 seconds
- Minnu centred, close-up, nothing important at the edges
